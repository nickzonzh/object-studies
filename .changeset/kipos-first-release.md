First release of Kipos: a Greek garden bed and whitewashed tins that grow over real days. `Bed` has three plots, seed packets for tomato, cucumber and watermelon, and a watering can; `Teneke` is a single tin of basil or geranium. Plants grow only while their soil is wet, wilt after a day without water and recover when watered. State is kept on the device, no login.
